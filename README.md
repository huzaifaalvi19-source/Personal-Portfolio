## Research

Where the question began, and what the data answered.

Published · Engineering Proceedings (MDPI), ICAME-25

### Proportional Analysis and Microstructural Characterization of Threading Dies Manufactured Using Conventional and EDM Machining Methods

Why do two dies cut from the same H13 steel perform so differently? I traced hardness and grain size through machining, heating, quenching and tempering, and found that EDM-made dies preserved structural integrity with minimal hardness variation. In service, they threaded **20 inches** of mild steel rod against **16 inches** for conventionally machined dies.
