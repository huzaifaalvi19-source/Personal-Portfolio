Project 01 / Circular manufacturing

Challenge. Recover useful material from failed prints while controlling filament consistency.

Approach. Integrate a 460 mm barrel, cartridge heaters, a 49E Hall-effect sensor and an L298N-controlled puller. Evaluate thermal, flow and structural behavior alongside experimental trials.

Design target. Approximately ±0.05 mm dimensional tolerance. This is a target, not a claim of measured production capability.

Next questions. Automated winding, process monitoring and recycling other thermoplastics.

04 / MDPI Research Publication

Published research: how machining changes a tool’s life.

Comparing H13 threading dies manufactured through conventional machining and electrical discharge machining.

Engineering Proceedings · Read published paper ↗

Explore the case study

Methods. Hardness testing, microstructural characterization and threading performance experiments.

Findings. The repository reports that the conventional die failed after 16 inches of threading; the EDM die threaded up to 20 inches, with lower hardness variation and greater microstructural stability under the study conditions.
